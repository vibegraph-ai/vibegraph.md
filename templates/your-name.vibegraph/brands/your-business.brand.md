# Your Business

> **How to fill this in.** A business brand lives inside its owner's vibegraph, beside the personal brand. It swaps the identity core for organizational foundations, because a company cannot take a personality test, then keeps the same twelve Brand Context and eight Brand Visuals elements. Rename this file `<business-slug>.brand.md`, update its line in VIBEGRAPH.md (with the business's tasks in the `on-task` hint), and delete the quote blocks as you go. No business?
