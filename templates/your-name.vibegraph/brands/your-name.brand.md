> **How to fill this in.** Two halves: Brand Context (the brand in words, twelve elements) and Brand Visuals (the brand in pictures, eight elements). Every element is a small, opinionated artifact written for a machine reader: "I help X do Y by Z" beats a paragraph of throat-clearing. Build it from your identity file. ### Unique Value Proposition

> One sentence, 20 to 50 words: I help [audience] [outcome] by [method]. Could a stranger repeat it back and have it still be true of only you?

### Purpose-Vision-Mission

> Purpose: "To [contribution] so that [impact]" (survives a pivot). Vision: the concrete future state. Mission: present tense, "I [activity] for [audience] through [method]." Chain test: "Because [purpose], I am building toward [vision], so today I [mission]" reads as one sane sentence.

### Tone & Voice

> Your constant voice (three or four adjectives you would defend), how tone shifts per channel, do rules, don't rules, signature phrases, and words you never use.

### Messaging & Narratives

> Three to five rows, each "The message itself: the story or reasoning that makes it believable." At least one carries your origin story and its turning point; at least one names the myth in your field you are against.

### Keywords & Phrases

> 8 to 15 terms at the intersection of who you are and what your audience actually searches. Ownable, not generic.
