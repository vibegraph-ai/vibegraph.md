# Ikigai

> **How to fill this in.** Four pillars, answered honestly and separately,
> then the places they genuinely meet, then one statement. Write in first
> person, with specifics: real activities, real people, real evidence.
> Resist the urge to make the four answers agree; the tension between them
> is information. Delete these quote blocks as you go.

## What I love

What you would still be doing on a Saturday with nobody watching. Two or
three sentences.

## What the world needs from me

The problems you feel pulled toward; who is underserved and how. Two or
three sentences.

## What I am naturally good at

Talents and tendencies, not just trained skills: what people come to you
for, what feels easy to you that others find hard.

## What I can be paid for

What people have actually paid you for, or credibly would. Keep it evidence-
based; money here is a signal, not the point.

## Where they meet

The intersections that hold up: love + needs (mission territory), love +
good at (passion territory), good at + paid (profession territory), needs +
paid (vocation territory). Note where a pillar is missing from your current
work.

## My Ikigai statement

One or two sentences that the rest of your vibegraph builds on. The test: a
stranger reading it should know what gets you up in the morning and why it
matters beyond you.
