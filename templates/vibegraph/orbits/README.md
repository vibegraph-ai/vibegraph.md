# Orbits

Orbits are the third part of a vibegraph: self-defined domains (health,
finances, career, operations) holding the documents, data, and protocols of
your life or business. They revolve around the core, and they stay home when
the core travels: an exported vibegraph carries pointer documents like the
ones in this folder, not the content itself. The content lives in your own
tools (an Obsidian vault, a Notion workspace, a folder of files), and
anything that serves it to an AI must honor the permission model in the
spec's Section 6.

Suggested starting orbits:

- **Personal:** Health & Wellness · Relationships · Finances · Career ·
  Notes & Ideas · Time Management · Skills · Tech Stack · Goals
- **Business:** Relationships (CRM) · Finances · Operations · Skills
  (Playbooks/SOPs) · Management · Brand Templates · Goals · Notes & Ideas ·
  Tech Stack

Rename, merge, split, and invent freely. Add an orbit only when a real use
case demands one; `goals.md` in this folder is a worked example of the
pointer shape. Delete what you don't need.
