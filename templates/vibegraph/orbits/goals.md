# Orbit: Goals

> **How to fill this in.** An orbit document in an export is a pointer: it
> tells a permitted consumer that the orbit exists, what it covers, and
> where the content lives. Keep the content itself in your own tool, and
> describe here only what you are comfortable with a permitted agent
> knowing about the orbit's shape. Delete these quote blocks as you go.

## What this orbit covers

Short- and long-term goals, reviewed on a daily/weekly/monthly/yearly
cadence.

## Where the content lives

Name the tool and location, e.g. "Obsidian vault, folder `Goals/`" or a
Notion workspace name. An agent granted this orbit reads there, subject to
your permissions; nothing in this file exposes the goals themselves.
