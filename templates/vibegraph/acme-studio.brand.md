# Business Brand: Acme Studio

> **How to fill this in.** A business brand lives inside its owner's
> vibegraph, beside the personal brand: one file per business you own,
> named `<business-slug>.brand.md`. It keeps the same Context and Visuals
> structure as the personal brand and swaps the identity foundations for
> organizational instruments, because a company cannot take a personality
> test. Rename this file to your business and delete the quote blocks as
> you go. If you have no business brand, delete this file and its line in
> the master read order.

## Brand Personality

Aaker's five dimensions (Sincerity, Excitement, Competence, Sophistication,
Ruggedness), each scored high/moderate/low with a note, plus the one
Jungian brand archetype the brand speaks from (Hero, Sage, Creator, Outlaw,
Caregiver, and so on) and how it shows up.

## Purpose: Why, How, What

The Golden Circle, plus conventional statements. Why: the belief the
business exists to advance (check it against the founder's personal Why in
this same vibegraph). How: the distinct approach. What: the products and
services. Then mission, vision, and values as the business states them.

## Brand Name

The business name and its naming rules.

## Slogans & Taglines

The lines the business repeats.

## Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

ONE sentence: we help [audience] [outcome] by [method].

## Purpose-Vision-Mission

The business's three statements, same shapes as the personal brand's, in
the company's voice.

## Core Values

Three to five rows, each as "Label: one falsifiable sentence of how the
business behaves."

## Tone & Voice

The brand's constant voice, per-channel shifts, do and don't rules, and
banned words.

## Messaging & Narratives

Key messages each paired with the story behind them: the origin story, the
category point of view, what the business is against.

## Keywords & Phrases

8 to 15 ownable terms for the category.

## Bio (Short/Long)

Boilerplates: one-liner, short (2 to 3 sentences), and full paragraph.

## Achievements & Awards

Proof: customers, numbers, milestones, press.

## Online Presence

Where the business exists publicly: platform, handle, primary channel.

## Symbols & Logos

The business logo system and usage rules.

## Color Palette

Roles and hex values.

## Typography

Typefaces and their jobs.

## Iconography

Icon style and set.

## Brand Imagery

Imagery direction for the business.

## Illustration Style

Illustration rules, or "none."

## Visual Elements

Recurring graphic devices and templates the business produces repeatedly
(social posts, decks, email signatures, landing pages).

## Photography

Photography direction for the business's surfaces.
