# Personality

> **How to fill this in.** The personality layer is a full assessment in two
> parts: the Big Five (dimensional trait scores) and the Enneagram (core
> motivations), reconciled in an integrated reading. Free, public-domain Big
> Five options exist at [ipip.ori.org](https://ipip.ori.org); the reference
> implementation uses the IPIP-NEO-120. Record your results, then write the
> interpretations in your own words: the scores give an AI calibration; the
> words tell it what the scores *mean for how you work and communicate.*
> Delete these quote blocks as you go.

## Part one: Big Five (OCEAN) scores

Record each dimension as a percentile or high/moderate/low. Add facet notes
where you know them.

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Openness** | | e.g. high: curious, abstract, drawn to novelty |
| **Conscientiousness** | | e.g. moderate: organized in bursts, deadline-driven |
| **Extraversion** | | e.g. low: reflective, energized by focus not crowds |
| **Agreeableness** | | e.g. moderate: warm but willing to disagree |
| **Neuroticism** | | e.g. low: even-keeled under pressure |

## Part two: Enneagram

Record your type, wing, and what actually drives you.

- **Type and wing:** e.g. Type 3w4
- **Core desire:** what you are always moving toward
- **Core fear:** what you organize your life to avoid
- **Under stress:** the pattern you fall into
- **In growth:** the pattern you reach for

## The integrated reading

Write 5 to 10 plain sentences reconciling the two: where the instruments
agree, where they tension, and what the combination means for how you work,
decide, and communicate. This is the section most AI consumers will actually
use; make every sentence something you would say about yourself.
