# Personality

> **How to fill this in.** Take a Big Five (OCEAN) assessment — a free, public-domain
> option is the [IPIP-based test](https://ipip.ori.org). Record your scores below,
> then write the interpretation in your own words. The scores give an AI calibration;
> the interpretation tells it what the scores *mean for how you work and communicate.*
> Delete these quote blocks as you go.

## Big Five scores

Record each dimension as a percentile or high/moderate/low. Add facet notes where you
know them.

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Openness** | — | e.g. high — curious, abstract, drawn to novelty |
| **Conscientiousness** | — | e.g. moderate — organized in bursts, deadline-driven |
| **Extraversion** | — | e.g. low — reflective, energized by focus not crowds |
| **Agreeableness** | — | e.g. moderate — warm but willing to disagree |
| **Neuroticism** | — | e.g. low — even-keeled under pressure |

## What this means in practice

Write 3–6 plain sentences translating the scores into working behavior. This is the
part an AI actually uses. For example:

- I think in systems and analogies; explanations that connect ideas land better than lists of facts.
- I'd rather ship a rough version and refine than plan exhaustively up front.
- I prefer directness. Don't hedge or over-qualify when writing as me.
- I stay calm in high-stakes moments; my tone doesn't need to convey urgency to convey importance.

## How I want AI to reflect this

One or two lines of explicit instruction. For example:
*"When writing as me, be direct and concrete, use analogies over jargon, and don't
manufacture enthusiasm I wouldn't have."*
