# Purpose & Meaning

> **How to fill this in.** This section codifies *why you do what you do* using
> [Ikigai 2.0](https://sloww.co/ikigai-2-0) — the corrected treatment of ikigai that
> separates purpose, meaning, and vocation instead of collapsing them into one career
> sweet-spot. Answer honestly and specifically; vague purpose statements produce vague
> AI output. Delete the guidance blocks as you go.

## What gets me up in the morning

The thing that energizes you, independent of money or recognition. Be concrete.

## What I find meaningful

What matters to you — the values and contributions that make effort feel worthwhile,
even when it's hard.

## What I'm good at

Your genuine strengths and hard-won skills. Not aspirational — actual.

## What the world needs from me

Where your strengths meet a real need. The problem you're positioned to help solve.

## How I make a living from this

The vocational reality — how the above translates (or is meant to translate) into work
and income.

## My purpose, in one line

Synthesize the above into a single sentence. This is the line an AI can reason from.
Example: *"I help people who feel unseen turn what makes them different into how they're
known."*

## Direction (next 1–3 years)

Where this purpose is taking you right now. What you're building toward.
