# Brand — Aesthetics

> **How to fill this in.** This is the visual half of your brand. Unlike positioning,
> it should produce *concrete artifacts* — actual hex values, real typeface names —
> not vague direction like "clean and modern." When an AI generates a slide, a
> thumbnail, or a landing page for you, this is what keeps it on-brand. Delete the
> guidance as you go.

## Aesthetic principles

2–4 sentences on the overall feel and the rules behind it. Example: *"Editorial and
restrained. Lots of whitespace, one accent color used sparingly, never more than two
typefaces. Warmth comes from the writing, not from decoration."*

## Logo & symbol

## Iconography & imagery

- **Icon style:** e.g. thin line, rounded, two-tone.
- **Imagery direction:** the kind of images that are "you" (and the kind that aren't).
- **Photography:** how you're shot — lighting, setting, wardrobe, mood — for consistent
  headshots and brand photos.
