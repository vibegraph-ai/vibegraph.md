# Brand — Positioning

> **How to fill this in.** This is the strategic half of your personal brand — the
> words. It's what lets an AI write *as you* rather than as a generic professional in
> your field. Be specific and opinionated; a positioning that could describe anyone
> describes no one. Delete the guidance as you go.

## Positioning statement

One or two sentences: who you help, what you help them do, and why you're the one to do
it. Example: *"I help solo founders sound like themselves in public, so their writing
builds trust instead of blending in."*

## Unique value proposition

The distinct value only you offer. What you do differently from everyone adjacent to you.

## Purpose, vision, mission

- **Purpose** — why you exist (pull from `core/purpose.md`).
- **Vision** — the future you're working toward.
- **Mission** — what you do, day to day, to get there.

## Core values

3–6 values that actually govern your choices. Add a phrase on what each looks like in
practice.

## Tone & voice

The most-used section. Describe how you sound, with specifics an AI can apply:

- **Voice (constant):** e.g. warm, direct, wry, generous with credit.
- **Tone (varies):** how you shift between, say, a LinkedIn post and a client email.
- **Do:** short sentences; concrete examples; second person; occasional dry humor.
- **Don't:** hedging, hype, exclamation points, corporate abstractions, em-dash pile-ups.
