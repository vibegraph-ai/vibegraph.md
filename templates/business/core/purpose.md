# Purpose — Why / How / What

> **How to fill this in.** In place of a personal purpose framework, the business Core
> uses Simon Sinek's **Golden Circle** alongside conventional mission/vision/values.
> Companies running on EOS can paste their Vision/Traction Organizer content here almost
> unchanged. Delete the guidance as you go.

## Why
The company's core belief and reason for existing, beyond making money. The cause the
brand advances. This is the line everything else should ladder up to.

## How
The distinctive approach, principles, or process that delivers the Why. What you do
differently from everyone solving the same problem.

## What
The actual products and services you offer — the tangible result of the Why and How.

## Mission
What the company does day to day to advance the Why.

## Vision
The future the company is working toward — where you want to be in 3–10 years.

## Values
3–6 values that actually govern decisions, each with a phrase on what it looks like in
practice (and, ideally, what violating it looks like).

## How I want AI to use this
e.g. "Frame all external messaging around the Why. Never lead with features; lead with
the belief, then show how the What delivers it."
