# Brand Personality

> **How to fill this in.** A company can't take a personality test, so the business Core
> uses two established brand instruments in place of the Big Five: **Jung's twelve
> archetypes** to choose a character, and **Aaker's five dimensions** to describe and
> measure it. Use them together — pick the archetype, then rate the dimensions. Delete
> the guidance as you go.

## Brand archetype

Choose the primary archetype that best fits the brand's character, and optionally a
secondary. The twelve: Innocent, Sage, Explorer, Outlaw, Magician, Hero, Lover, Jester,
Everyman, Caregiver, Ruler, Creator.
