# Brand — Positioning

> **How to fill this in.** The strategic half of the brand — the words that let an AI
> write *as the company*. Be specific; positioning that could describe any competitor
> describes nothing. Delete the guidance as you go.

## Positioning statement
For [target customer] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit], because
[reason to believe]. Write the real version.

## Unique value proposition
The distinct value only this company offers.

## Target customer / ICP
Who you serve — and, just as usefully, who you don't.

## Category
The market frame you compete in (and any frame you deliberately reject).
