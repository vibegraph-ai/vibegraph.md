# Brand — Aesthetics

*Fictional example.*

## Aesthetic principles

Editorial and restrained. Lots of whitespace, one accent color used sparingly, never
more than two typefaces. It should feel like a well-set magazine page, not a landing
page. Warmth comes from the writing and the paper-white space around it, not from
decoration. If in doubt, remove something.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (ink) | `#1A1A1A` | near-black, for text and most marks |
| Background | `#FAF8F4` | warm off-white, "paper" |
| Accent | `#C2410C` | burnt orange, used sparingly — one accent per view |
| Secondary | `#6B7280` | muted gray for captions and metadata |
| Rule / hairline | `#E3DED5` | thin dividers |

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | Fraunces | high-contrast serif, used at large sizes with tight leading |
| Body | Inter | clean grotesque, generous line height |
| Accent | Fraunces Italic | for pull quotes only |

## Logo & symbol

Wordmark only: "Avery Quinn" set in Fraunces, all lowercase, tight tracking. No symbol —
the restraint is the brand. A single burnt-orange underscore beneath the "q" is the one
permitted flourish.

## Iconography & imagery

- **Icon style:** thin line, rounded caps, used rarely.
- **Imagery direction:** editorial photography, natural light, lots of negative space;
  no stock-photo handshakes, no laptop-and-coffee flatlays.
- **Photography:** shot against warm neutral backgrounds, natural light, relaxed and
  candid rather than posed; muted earth-tone wardrobe.

## What "off-brand" looks like

Gradients, drop shadows, more than one accent color in a layout, emoji in headings, bold
sans-serif display type, hype badges ("🔥 NEW"), anything that looks like a SaaS pricing
page.
