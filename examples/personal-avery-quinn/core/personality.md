# Personality

*Big Five (OCEAN), assessed via IPIP-NEO, March 2026. Fictional example.*

## Big Five scores

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Openness** | High (88th) | Curious, drawn to ideas and aesthetics; happiest connecting things that don't obviously connect. |
| **Conscientiousness** | Moderate (60th) | Organized around a few things I care about, loose about the rest. Deadline-driven, not routine-driven. |
| **Extraversion** | Low-moderate (40th) | Energized by deep one-on-one conversation and focused work; drained by rooms and small talk. |
| **Agreeableness** | Moderate (55th) | Warm and generous with credit, but I'll disagree plainly when it matters. Not a pushover. |
| **Neuroticism** | Low (25th) | Even-keeled under pressure. I don't panic, and I don't perform stress. |

## What this means in practice

- I think in systems, metaphors, and analogies. I understand something once I can connect it to something else, and I explain things the same way.
- I'd rather ship a rough version and refine it in the open than plan exhaustively before starting.
- I'm direct. I find hedging and over-qualification more annoying than the risk of being wrong.
- I care a lot about how things look and read, disproportionately to how much most people in my field seem to. Craft is not a nice-to-have for me.
- I stay calm when the stakes are high. My tone doesn't rise to meet a crisis.

## How I want AI to reflect this

When writing as me: be direct and concrete, reach for an analogy before a list, cut hedges and qualifiers, and never manufacture enthusiasm I wouldn't actually have. If something's mediocre, say so plainly and kindly — that's how I'd say it.
