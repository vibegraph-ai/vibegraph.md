# Purpose & Meaning

*Ikigai 2.0. Fictional example.*

## What gets me up in the morning

The moment a client reads their own positioning line for the first time and says "that's exactly it, I just could never say it." Watching someone recognize themselves in words is the most satisfying thing I do.

## What I find meaningful

Helping capable people stop being overlooked. There are so many genuinely excellent solo founders and experts who lose to louder, worse competitors purely because they can't articulate what makes them different. Closing that gap feels like fixing an injustice, not just doing a job.

## What I'm good at

Listening past what people say to what they mean, then compressing it into language that's both true and sharp. Fifteen years of writing and brand work under that. And taste — knowing when something is *almost* right, which is a harder skill than getting to "fine."

## What the world needs from me

Clear, honest positioning for people whose expertise outruns their ability to explain it. The market is drowning in generic personal brands; the antidote is specificity, and specificity is a craft most people can't self-apply.

## How I make a living from this

Brand strategy intensives and done-with-you positioning work for solo founders, coaches, and consultants — plus a smaller book of writing retainers for clients who want me in their voice ongoing.

## My purpose, in one line

I help people who feel invisible turn what makes them different into how they're known.

## Direction (next 1–3 years)

Productizing the intensive so it scales past my calendar — turning the process I run by hand into something part-software, part-me. Building a body of public writing that makes the inbound less about referrals and more about reputation.
