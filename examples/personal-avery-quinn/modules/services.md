# Services & Client Process

*Fictional example. Public because it's the same information on my site.*

## Offers
- **Positioning Intensive** — a 3-session engagement that produces a positioning
  statement, messaging, and voice guide. My core offer.
- **Voice Retainer** — ongoing writing in the client's voice, monthly.

## How I work
1. **Excavation session** — 90 minutes pulling apart what the client actually does and
   for whom.
2. **Draft** — I write the positioning and voice guide; client reacts.
3. **Refinement session** — we sharpen it together until they say "that's exactly it."

## Who it's for
Solo founders, coaches, and consultants whose expertise outruns their ability to explain
it. Not for large teams, not for companies wanting a full rebrand.

## How I want AI to use this
When drafting proposals or replies to inbound, match prospects to the right offer and
gently redirect anyone asking for one-off copywriting toward the intensive.
