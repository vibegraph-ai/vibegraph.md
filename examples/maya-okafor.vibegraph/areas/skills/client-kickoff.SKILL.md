# Client kickoff

Voice: follow `brands/maya-okafor.brand.md` for anything the client reads. Calm, specific, no exclamation points.

1. Confirm the signed scope and the start date in the client record.
2. Duplicate the Delivery OS project template and name it `<client-slug>-<yyyy-mm>`.
3. Send the kickoff email from the template. Subject: "Kickoff: what happens this week."
4. Book a 45-minute kickoff call within five business days.
5. On the call, walk through the one-page delivery plan and agree the Friday update rhythm.
6. Send the first-week checklist within two hours of the call.

Done when: the client has the plan, the checklist, and the next three Friday updates on their calendar.
