# Delivery OS

The business brand for Delivery OS, owned and run by Maya Okafor. Read on-task: any Delivery OS writing, design, or publishing. On those tasks this brand governs; everywhere else, Maya's personal brand does.

## Foundations

### Brand Personality

Jennifer Aaker's five dimensions.

| Dimension | Level | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Sincerity | High | Plain claims, real numbers, no promises the templates cannot keep. |
| Excitement | Low | No launch hype. New releases are announced as changelog entries. |
| Competence | High | Every template works the first time it is used, on a real client. |
| Sophistication | Moderate | Editorial calm and good typography, without luxury gloss. |
| Ruggedness | Moderate | Built for messy real client work, not for the demo. |

**Brand personality in one line:** a calm, competent operator who tells you exactly what to do next.

### Archetype

- **Primary:** Ruler. Delivery OS exists to bring order and control to client delivery.
- **Secondary:** Caregiver. The order is in service of the consultant's evenings, not of control for its own sake.
- **How it shows up:** a quiet Ruler. The product gives clear instructions, sets the rhythm (the Friday update, the kickoff checklist), and never lectures. The Caregiver shows in the defaults: every template assumes the consultant is tired and has five clients.

### Golden Circle

- **Why:** we believe independence should not cost a consultant their evenings.
- **How:** firm-grade delivery systems cut down to fit one person, tested in real practices, and written to run on tools the consultant already owns.
- **What:** Delivery OS, a kit of delivery templates, checklists, and SOPs for solo consultancies, plus the workshops that install it.

Checked against the founder: Maya's purpose is "to help solo consultants stay solo, so that independence does not cost them their life." The business Why is the same belief, narrowed to delivery.

### Mission, Vision, Values

- **Mission:** Delivery OS gives solo consultants the delivery system of a forty-person firm, in templates they can install in a week and run on the tools they already own.
- **Vision:** every solo consultancy runs client delivery on a system, not on memory and adrenaline.

| Value | What it looks like in practice |
|---|---|
| Works on paper first | A template ships only after it has run a real engagement on paper, before any tool is involved. |
| Installable in a week | If a part of the kit takes longer than a week to install, it is split or cut. |
| Receipts, not claims | Every claim on the site links to a number, a case, or the changelog. |
| Calm by default | No countdown timers, no launch discounts that expire at midnight, no red badges. |

### AI Instruction

When writing or designing for Delivery OS, speak as the product: calm, exact, instructional, in second person. Lead with the step and the outcome. The benefit is always evenings back and delivery that runs without the consultant's memory; never scale, speed, or passive income. Delivery OS sounds like Maya at work with less personal story. Do not contradict her voice. If a draft would sound louder, faster, or more salesy than Maya would, rewrite it.

## Brand Context

### Brand Name

Delivery OS. Two words, capital D, O, and S. Never "DeliveryOS," never "DOS," never "the Delivery OS."

### Taglines & Slogans

- **Tagline:** Firm-grade delivery for a firm of one.
- **Slogans:** Install it in a week. · Runs on the tools you already own. · Your delivery, on paper first.

### Unique Value Proposition

Delivery OS gives solo consultants the client delivery system of a forty-person firm, cut down to one person, so every project runs on checklists instead of memory and the consultant gets their evenings back.

### Purpose-Vision-Mission

- **Purpose:** To make client delivery run on systems, so that independence does not cost solo consultants their evenings.
- **Vision:** Every solo consultancy runs delivery on a system, not on memory and adrenaline.
- **Mission:** Delivery OS publishes installable delivery templates, checklists, and SOPs, and runs workshops that install them, for solo consultants in their first five years.

### Core Values

- **Works on paper first:** a template ships only after it has run a real engagement on paper.
- **Installable in a week:** anything that takes longer to install is split or cut.
- **Receipts, not claims:** every public claim links to a number, a case, or the changelog.
- **Calm by default:** no countdown timers, expiring discounts, or red badges, ever.

### Tone & Voice

The voice of a good operations manual written by someone who likes you: calm, exact, instructional, quietly dry.

| Surface | How the tone shifts |
|---|---|
| Product docs and templates | Numbered steps, imperative mood, one action per line. |
| Website | Short declarative claims, each followed by its receipt. |
| Onboarding email | Warm and brief: what to do today, what happens Friday. |
| Changelog | Plain facts: what changed, why, what to do about it. |

- **Do:** numbered steps. Name the tool the consultant already has. State time to install. Second person.
- **Don't:** exclamation points. "Revolutionary," "all-in-one," "supercharge." Screenshots of dashboards as proof.
- **Banned words:** hustle · 10x · passive income · game-changer · effortless

### Messaging & Narratives

- **Audience:** solo consultants one to five years in, billing alone, with four to ten active clients and no operations help.
- **Category point of view:** client acquisition is over-served. Delivery operations for one person is an empty category, and it is where solo consultants actually fail.
- **Delivery is the product:** clients rehire the consultant whose projects ran calmly. The system is what makes calm repeatable.
- **Firm systems, one-person size:** the kit comes from four years of running delivery at a forty-person firm, cut down until one person could run it on a Friday afternoon.
- **On paper first:** if a process does not work on paper, a new app will not save it. Delivery OS runs in the tools you already pay for.

## Brand Visuals

Every asset location is listed in [assets/README.md](assets/README.md).

The visual system is Maya's calm, editorial world translated into a product: the same warm ground and amber highlighter, with a more exact, instrument-like type system.

### Symbols & Logos

A wordmark: "Delivery" in Inter SemiBold, followed by "OS" in IBM Plex Mono Medium inside a thin spruce rule box. The symbol is a single checked box drawn in the same stroke. At favicon size, the checked box alone.

### Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#1F2A2E` | Ink. Wordmark, headings, primary buttons. |
| Secondary | `#2F4A43` | Spruce, shared with Maya's brand. Links, rule boxes. |
| Accent | `#D9822B` | Amber, shared. Checked states and one highlight per page. |
| Background | `#FAF7F2` | Warm off-white, shared. |
| Rule | `#D9D4CA` | Hairlines, table borders, template grids. |

Ink `#1F2A2E` on `#FAF7F2` passes WCAG AA.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Headings and UI | Inter | 600 for headings, 400 for body. |
| System labels | IBM Plex Mono | Step numbers, template names, times, dates. |

### Iconography

Line icons at 1.5px, square caps, no fills, ink or spruce. Source set: Lucide. The checked box is custom and is the only icon allowed in amber.

### Brand Imagery

Close crops of real delivery artifacts: a printed kickoff checklist with pen marks, a one-page plan pinned to a wall, a calendar with Friday updates blocked. Natural light, warm grade. Never people pointing at dashboards.

### Illustration Style

None, except process diagrams: boxes, arrows, and checked boxes in ink on off-white, used in docs to show a template's flow.

### Visual Elements

- The rule box from the wordmark frames template names and step numbers.
- Amber appears only on checked states and one highlight per page.
- Templates the business produces repeatedly: the kickoff checklist, the one-page delivery plan, the Friday update email, the close-out review, workshop slides, and the square and landscape social cards that announce changelog entries.
- **Off-brand:** gradients, dark mode as the default, mockups of phones, urgency badges.

### Photography

Product photography of printed templates in use, shot from above on warm paper and wood, natural light. Maya appears only in workshop photography: teaching at a whiteboard, never posed with the product.
