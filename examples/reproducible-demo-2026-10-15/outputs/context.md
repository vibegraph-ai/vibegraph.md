Most solo consultants fail not from lack of skill, but from letting client updates slip.

When you don't send a regular update, clients assume the project has stalled. Silence creates anxiety, and anxious clients send mid-week emails asking for status. That breaks your focus and steals your evenings.

Sending one update at the same time every week buys back your calm. Keep it brief and cover three things:

1. **What got done:** State the completed deliverables with real numbers and specific tools.
2. **What comes next:** Name the exact goal or end state for the upcoming week.
3. **What is needed:** List any missing inputs or decisions required from the client to keep moving.

Boring systems beat weekly firefighting. A predictable cadence builds trust and establishes your delivery operations like a firm ten times your size.

**Action to take today:** Open your calendar and block thirty minutes every Friday at 4:00 PM to send your weekly updates.
