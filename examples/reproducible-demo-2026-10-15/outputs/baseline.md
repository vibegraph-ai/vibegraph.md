As a solo operations consultant, silence can quietly erode client trust. When you are deep in execution, clients are left wondering what is happening behind the scenes.

The antidote is simple: send one weekly client update.

This single weekly touchpoint keeps momentum high, reinforces your value, and prevents unnecessary check-in emails.

Every update should cover three essential things:

* **What was accomplished** since the last message.
* **What is planned** for the week ahead.
* **Any roadblocks or decisions** needed from their side.

Consistency transforms how clients perceive your reliability. When they see a clear, structured summary landing in their inbox every week, confidence grows naturally.

**Practical action you can take today:** Draft a simple template containing these three sections and schedule your first weekly update for your current clients this Friday.
