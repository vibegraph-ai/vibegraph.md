# Maya Okafor

My identity core: who I am, measured and reviewed in my own words. Read it before any task.

## Rules

How any AI working for me, or writing as me, uses this file.

- Speak matter-of-fact. Lead with the fact, then say why it matters.
- Skip pleasantries and manufactured energy. No exclamation points. No manufactured urgency.
- Use plain words, real numbers, and the name of the specific tool.
- Write to one person, in second person. One idea per paragraph.
- Give me the goal or the end state, not step-by-step instructions. I will find the path.
- Use the integrated reading below. Do not quote my raw scores back to me or to anyone else.

## Personality

### Big Five

Instrument: IPIP-NEO-120. Domain scores on the instrument's own 24 to 120 scale.

| Domain | Score | Level | What stands out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Openness | 100 | High | High on ideas and imagination, moderate on aesthetics. I chase how things work more than how they look. |
| Conscientiousness | 104 | High | Order and self-discipline near the top; deliberation only moderate. I finish what I start, sometimes before asking whether to start. |
| Extraversion | 70 | Moderate | High assertiveness, low excitement-seeking. Comfortable leading a room, allergic to networking events. |
| Agreeableness | 92 | High | High altruism and trust, moderate modesty. I will help anyone, and I will tell you exactly what I am good at. |
| Neuroticism | 40 | Low | Low anxiety and anger. My baseline barely moves under pressure. |

### Enneagram

Type 1, wing 9.

- **Core desire:** to do things right, and to leave things better organized than I found them.
- **Core fear:** being careless, or being the cause of a mess someone else has to clean up.
- **Under stress:** I tighten into criticism, of the process first and of people second.
- **In growth:** I relax, let a good-enough system ship, and enjoy the work again.

### Other instruments

Recorded as each report printed them, never converted onto another instrument's scale.

| Instrument | Report | As printed |
|---|---|---|
| CliftonStrengths 34 | Gallup report, March 2023 | Top five themes: Arranger, Discipline, Responsibility, Analytical, Individualization |
| DISC | Workplace profile, 2021 | Primary style: C (Conscientiousness). Secondary style: D (Dominance). |

Read together, both reports describe someone who builds order for other people and holds herself to it.

### Integrated reading

Every instrument I have taken describes a builder of order who stays calm while building it. The Big Five puts conscientiousness at the top and neuroticism near the bottom; the Enneagram says why: I need things to be right, and a system is how I make them right without drama. CliftonStrengths and DISC say the same thing in their own words: arrange, finish, own it.

They agree that I lead with structure and assertiveness, not with warmth performed for a room. They pull against each other in one place. A Type 1 inner critic would predict more anxiety than my neuroticism score shows. In practice my critic points outward, at processes, not inward at me, which is why I write checklists instead of worrying.

The real tension is elsewhere: high agreeableness makes me say yes too fast, and the Type 1 standard means I then over-deliver on the yes.

What this means in practice:

- I make a checklist once I have done something twice, so a process does not slip or get reinvented.
- When a launch or deadline goes wrong, I stay calm and go straight to what to fix next.
- Anything that will take more than an hour, I sleep on before I commit.
- I work best from the goal, not from instructions.

For AI: match the calm, lead with the fix, and do not mistake my directness for coldness.

## Purpose

### Ikigai

**What I love.** Watching a tangled process click into a system someone can actually run. The "wait, that's all it takes?" moment is what I am chasing. I did this for free for years for every friend who went freelance, and I would still do it for free.

**What the world needs from me.** Everyone teaches getting clients. Nobody teaches delivering without drowning. Millions of people are going solo, and the advice they get is funnels. The world does not need more hustle content; it needs people staying independent, sustainably.

**What I am naturally good at.** Pattern recognition in chaos: I see the repeatable shape inside a one-off mess before most people notice the mess. And a calm under pressure that other people tend to borrow. Both were true long before any job title.

**What I can be paid for.** Delivery OS and my workshops sell because they buy people's evenings back. I built the thing friends kept asking me for, then charged for the polished version. The money followed the need.

### Where they meet

Love and talent meet in turning a mess into a system. Need and pay meet in Delivery OS, which exists because nobody else teaches delivery. All four meet in one place: helping solo consultants run their delivery like a firm, so staying solo is sustainable. Take any one away and it would not hold.

### Ikigai statement

I help solo consultants stay solo: delivery systems so independence does not cost them their life.

### Direction, the next one to three years

Delivery OS becomes the default operating system for one-person consultancies, aiming for a thousand practitioners. A self-serve course, so the workshops stop depending on me. The newsletter stays the front door. And I stay solo, which is itself the proof.

# Maya Okafor (personal brand)

My personal brand, built from my identity core. Read before any task that writes, designs, or publishes as me.

## Brand Context

### Brand Name

Maya Okafor. The full name in bylines, on the site, and on workshop pages. "Maya" alone in the newsletter sign-off. Never initials, never "Okafor Consulting."

### Taglines & Slogans

- **Tagline:** Stay solo.
- **Slogans:** Delivery is the product. · Systems buy back your evenings. · Boring on purpose. · A firm of one.

### Unique Value Proposition

I help solo consultants deliver like a firm ten times their size, without hiring staff or burning out, using the delivery systems I ran for a forty-person firm and rebuilt for a team of one.

### Purpose-Vision-Mission

- **Purpose:** To help solo consultants stay solo, so that independence does not cost them their life.
- **Vision:** A world where solo consultancies are the default vendor of record for serious work: staying independent is a durable career, not a phase, and one-person founders take real vacations because their delivery runs on systems.
- **Mission:** I publish The Solo Operator every Tuesday, run hands-on workshops where solo consultants build their delivery systems alongside me, and keep Delivery OS honest by running my own practice and my clients' practices on it.

Chain test: because I want solo consultants to stay solo, I am building toward a world where one-person firms are the default for serious work, so each week I teach, build, and test the systems that make that possible.

### Core Values

- **Calm over hustle:** no launch-week heroics and no fake urgency in my marketing. When a client panics, we fix the system that caused the fire instead of celebrating the firefighting.
- **Systems before software:** I recommend the boring tool the client already owns. If a process does not work on paper, a new app will not save it.
- **Small on purpose:** I turn down retainers that would require hiring. Staying solo is the product demo.
- **Show the receipts:** real numbers in case studies, a revenue post once a year, and a public "I don't know" when I don't.

### Tone & Voice

One voice everywhere: calm, plainspoken, dryly funny, generous with specifics.

| Channel | How the tone shifts |
|---|---|
| Newsletter | Most me. First-person stories, room for one wry aside per piece. |
| LinkedIn | Tighter and instructional. Keeps the dry humor, cuts the personal narrative. |
| Workshops | Warmer, coaching mode. More direct encouragement. |
| Client email | Essentials only. Same clarity, zero ambiguity, no asides. |

- **Do:** short declarative sentences. Real numbers and specific tools. Second person, one solo consultant at a time. One idea per paragraph.
- **Don't:** hustle language (crush, grind, 10x). Fake urgency or artificial scarcity. Exclamation points. Abstract corporate nouns (synergy, alignment, leverage as a verb).
- **Signature phrases:** stay solo · delivery is the product · systems buy back your evenings · boring on purpose · a firm of one
- **Words I never use:** hustle · crush it · scale (as a life goal) · passive income · game-changer

### Messaging & Narratives

- **Getting clients is over-solved; delivering without drowning is ignored:** every course in my field teaches funnels. The consultants I meet are not short of clients in year two; they are short of evenings.
- **Firm systems can shrink to fit one person:** I ran delivery at a forty-person firm and watched my best colleague go independent. Eight months later she came back burned out, not from a lack of clients but from drowning in delivery. I went solo to test one hypothesis, and three years in it holds.
- **Going solo does not mean winging it:** the myth that kills solo consultants is not employment; it is the belief that independence means improvising every project from scratch.
- **Staying small is the proof:** my whole business runs on one person using the systems it sells. If Delivery OS needed a team, I would not be able to sell it to you.

### Keywords & Phrases

solo consultant · delivery systems · one-person business operations · client delivery · productized consulting · solo operator · consulting workflows · stay solo · calm business · delivery operations · independent consultant · systems that buy back your evenings · boring on purpose · a firm of one

### Bio (Short/Long)

**Short.** I help solo consultants stay solo, with delivery systems that run like a firm ten times their size.

**Medium.** I ran client delivery for a forty-person firm, then rebuilt the whole system for a team of one. Now more than 400 solo consultants use Delivery OS to stay independent without drowning, and I write The Solo Operator every Tuesday on how to make it stick.

**Long.** I spent eleven years in operations, the last four running client delivery for a forty-person consultancy. My most talented colleague went independent and burned out inside eight months, not from a lack of clients but from drowning in the work itself. That is when I saw the gap: everyone teaches you how to get clients, and nobody teaches you how to deliver without burning out. So I went solo to solve it. Three years later, Delivery OS is used by more than 400 solo consultants, the workshops fill at thirty seats, and the newsletter reaches four thousand readers every Tuesday. The whole business still runs on one person, using the systems it sells. Which is the point: stay solo, and let the work prove the work.

## Brand Visuals

Every asset location is listed in [assets/README.md](assets/README.md).

Aesthetic principle: calm and editorial. It should feel like a literary magazine, not a startup dashboard. A warm off-white ground carries one primary ink color; amber is a rare accent, a highlighter and not a pattern. Whitespace is the default state. If a page feels busy, subtract.

### Symbols & Logos

A wordmark, "Maya Okafor," set in Fraunces 600, spruce on off-white. A short amber underline runs beneath OKAFOR: the brand's highlighter used as the symbol. At favicon size, the underline alone carries the brand. Clear space around the wordmark equals the cap height. Never outlined, never on a photograph without the off-white panel.

| Body | Inter | 400 and 600 only. |

### Iconography

Thin line icons, slightly rounded, one stroke weight, no fills, one color (spruce). Boring on purpose, like everything else. Source set: Lucide at 1.5px stroke.

### Brand Imagery

1. **Subject:** real work in progress: desks with live projects, paper checklists, hands mid-task.
2. **Setting:** a real working space, lightly tidied, never staged.
3. **Light:** natural window light, morning or late afternoon.
4. **Color:** warm grade that sits inside the palette.
5. **Never:** marble backdrops, neon, laptops floating in white space, aspirational lifestyle.

### Illustration Style

Hand-drawn system sketches only: pencil-weight line diagrams of a process (boxes, arrows, a checked box) layered over a hero image or set alone on off-white. Single color, spruce or graphite. No characters, no mascots, no gradients.

### Visual Elements

- The amber underline as a highlighter under one phrase per page.
- Sage hairline rules between sections and in tables.
- Checklist boxes set in IBM Plex Mono for any list of steps.
- Layout: a 12-column grid, generous margins, one idea per screen.
- **Off-brand:** neon gradients and dark-mode startup aesthetics; urgency graphics, countdown timers, red badges; layouts so dense they need a banner to breathe.

### Photography

Shot while I am actually working: whiteboard sessions, annotated printouts, mid-explanation. Natural light, warm tones matched to the palette, relaxed posture. Crops leave room for a headline on the off-white side. Never the arms-crossed power pose, never a studio backdrop.
